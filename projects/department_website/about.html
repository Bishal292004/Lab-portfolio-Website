WHO WE ARE

Building the next generation of computing professionals

The Department of Computer Science at Pondicherry University is dedicated to providing students with a strong foundation in computer science, programming, software development and emerging technologies.

The department focuses on creating an environment where students can develop their technical skills, analytical thinking and problem-solving abilities.

Through academic learning, practical activities and research opportunities, students are encouraged to explore the rapidly changing world of technology.
